Image Map Color Guide

Use these colors in your image maps for automatic terrain detection:

Water
RGB(70, 130, 180) - Steel Blue
Forest
RGB(34, 139, 34) - Forest Green
Plains
RGB(144, 238, 144) - Light Green
Mountains
RGB(105, 105, 105) - Dim Gray
Desert
RGB(244, 164, 96) - Sandy Brown
Hills
RGB(143, 188, 143) - Dark Sea Green
Swamp
RGB(85, 107, 47) - Dark Olive Green
Tundra
RGB(240, 248, 255) - Alice Blue

Tips:

  • Colors don't need to be exact - there's a 50-pixel tolerance
  • Use image editing software like GIMP, Paint.NET, or Photoshop
  • Transparent areas will be ignored
  • Smaller images work better for performance
